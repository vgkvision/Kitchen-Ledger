Kitchen Ledger Plus BETA

Notes for the family test. The original Kitchen Ledger icon is unchanged. These additions live only in the KL Plus icon.

Open Plus from the gold KL Plus icon. If that icon was already on the phone, close it all the way and open it again so this beta loads. On a computer, use the Plus page. The pictures below use the sample cookbook. After you restore your backup, the same buttons are there with your own dishes.

1 · Your name

Changes can say who made them

Type a name in the banner under the title and tap Save. A new dish, a planned dinner, and a grocery item will carry that name. Each phone keeps its own name.

2 · Want to try

A shelf for ideas

  1. Open the Want to try tab.
  2. Save a dish name, a link, and one line about why it might fit.
  3. When you want to cook it, pick a category and tap Add to rotation.

The full recipe stays on the website. Plus only keeps the name, the link, and your note.

3 · Added to the rotation

The idea becomes a real dish

After you add it to the rotation, the dish shows who saved the idea. You can fill in the recipe later, the same way as any other dish.

4 · Repeat a past week

Copy a week you already liked

  1. Open Menu Planner and look at the week you want to fill.
  2. Tap Repeat a past week.
  3. Tap the earlier week. Its dinners land on the same days.

If the week on screen already has dinners, Plus asks before it replaces them.

5 · The copied week

The name stays on the plan

Each copied dinner shows the name of the person who repeated the week.

6 · Favorites

Fill the open days

Heart a dish in Rotation, or mark that Jeff likes it. Then, on the Menu Planner, tap Fill open days with favorites. Days that already have a dinner stay as they are. Plus picks favorites you have not already used that week, starting with the ones made least recently.

7 · Store mode

A list you can read in the aisle

  1. Open Shopping List.
  2. Tap Store mode.
  3. Type gets larger, checked items hide, and the “pull from your menu” section tucks away.
  4. Tap Leave store mode when you are home.

A new item can say who added it, such as “Jeff added this.”

8 · Photos

Pictures stay on the phone that added them

A photo you add still shows on that phone. For this beta it is not sent up with the family share, so a big picture is less likely to trip the size warning. Everyone still shares the recipes, the menu, the shopping list, and the Want to try shelf. A backup you download on that phone still includes its photos.

Sharing Plus with the family

Inside KL Plus, open Share with family and tap Start sharing. That makes a new sync code. Send the family the Plus link and that new code. The same API key from jsonbin.io is fine.

Keep the original Kitchen Ledger sync code in the original app. Keep the Plus code in Plus.

Help inside Plus also lists these steps. This page is saved with the app, so you can open it again from the Plus folder.
